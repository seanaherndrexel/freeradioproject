Privacy

This site collects only what you type into its forms: your name, contact details, organization and message. We use it to reply to you, to manage the list for free ads, and to talk with possible sponsors.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use advertising trackers on this site. Form entries are stored in a private spreadsheet that only The Free Radio Project can open.

To see or delete what we hold about you, send a message through the contact form.
