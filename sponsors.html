Your Name Behind Every Sponsorship

When you sponsor, you pay for the airtime behind a local group's free ad. In return, listeners hear your business thanked by name, and we add you to our sponsor wall with a link to your site.

On the air it sounds like this: “Airtime made possible by Smith Hardware of Doylestown.” Federal rules for nonprofits let us thank you by name, location and slogan, but they don't allow product comparisons or sales pitches, so every mention stays a simple thank-you.

LevelGiftWhat you receive
Neighbor$500 a yearYour name and link on the sponsor wall, and a thank-you post.
Supporter$2,500 a yearEverything above, and an on-air thank-you on 12 free ads a year.
Champion$7,500 a yearEverything above, an on-air thank-you on one in four free ads, and a quarterly report of the causes you helped.
Presenting$20,000 a year“The Free Radio Project, presented by” your name on every free ad for a year, and the top of the sponsor wall.
Fund an ad$250 oncePays for one cause's ad from script to airtime, with your name on that ad's story page.
Founding sponsors are the first 25 businesses to sign on. Their price stays locked for three years, and they're marked as founders on the sponsor wall for good. We aren't taking payments yet, so for now just send the form and we'll call you to talk it through.
