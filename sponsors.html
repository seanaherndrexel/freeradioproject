Your Name Behind Every Sponsorship

When you sponsor, you pay for the airtime behind a local cause. Listeners hear your business thanked by name.

What your sponsorship does

Every free ad we make is paid for by a sponsor. Your gift covers the script, the recording and the airtime for a cause that could never buy an ad of its own.

In return, listeners in your own community hear your business thanked by name, and we add you to our sponsor wall with a link to your site.

“Airtime made possible by Smith Hardware of Doylestown.”

Federal rules for nonprofits let us thank you by name and location. They do not allow prices, comparisons or sales pitches, so every mention stays a simple thank-you.

Questions sponsors ask

Will listeners hear my name?

Yearly sponsors at the Supporter level and above are thanked by name on the air. A one-time gift puts your name on that ad's story page.

What does the on-air thank-you sound like?

It is one short line at the end of the ad, such as “Airtime made possible by Smith Hardware of Doylestown.” It names you and where you are, and it never includes prices or a sales pitch.

Can I pick a cause that is not on the list?

Sponsors choose from causes that have applied and been approved. If you know a group that should be on the list, send them to the application.
