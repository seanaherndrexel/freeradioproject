A good cause should not need an ad budget

A lot of groups doing good work are unknown a mile from their own front door. Advertising would change that, but it's usually the first thing a small organization can't afford.

The Free Radio Project exists to close that gap. We write a 30-second ad for a group, record it and put it on the air at no cost to them, and local sponsors pay for the airtime. Our founder, Sean Ahern, is an audio engineer and copywriter in Doylestown, Pennsylvania, who has spent years producing radio ads.

What we will not air

We don't air ads for or against political candidates, ads that make false claims, or adult content. To keep things fair, our sponsors and board members can't receive free ads either.
