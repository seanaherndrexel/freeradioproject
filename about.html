A good cause should not need an ad budget

We write, record and air radio ads for local causes, and local sponsors pay for the airtime.

The gap we close

A lot of groups doing good work are unknown a mile from their own front door. Advertising would change that, but it is usually the first thing a small organization cannot afford.

The Free Radio Project exists to close that gap. We write a 30-second ad for a cause, record it and put it on the air at no cost to them. Local sponsors pay for the airtime, and listeners hear those sponsors thanked by name.

See how it works

What we promise

Free to every cause

A cause never pays for the script, the recording or the airtime.

Your words, your approval

Nothing is recorded until the cause has read the script and said yes.

Sponsors choose, causes come first

Sponsors pick from causes that have applied. They cannot add their own, and they cannot receive free ads.

A simple thank-you

Sponsors are thanked by name. We never turn a cause's ad into a sales pitch.

No politics

We do not air anything for or against a candidate, a party or a ballot question.

Plain dealing

We are still being formed as a nonprofit, and we say so on every page until the paperwork is done.

Our founder

Sean Ahern is an audio engineer and copywriter in Doylestown, Pennsylvania, who has spent years producing radio ads. He holds a degree in audio engineering and an MBA from Drexel University, has written for more than 400 clients, and has been a musician for over 15 years.

He started The Free Radio Project because the people who can afford advertising are usually the only ones who get heard.

The Free Radio Project is separate from his businesses and buys no services from them.
