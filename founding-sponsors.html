A locked price

Whatever level you choose today stays at today's price for three years, even if our levels change.

Permanent recognition

Your business is marked as a founding sponsor on our sponsor wall for as long as the wall exists.

The first ads carry your name

Founders at the Supporter level and above are thanked by name on the first ads we put on the air.

Why the first sponsors count most

A cause cannot get its ad until someone pays for the airtime. The first sponsors are the reason the first food pantry, fire company and youth league are heard at all.

Every founding level is a regular sponsor level with the founder terms added, so you choose the amount that suits your business.

Claim a founding place

We are not taking payments yet. Send the form to hold a place, and we will call or email you within two business days.

How it works

  • Send the form to hold your place
  • We call to talk through the levels
  • You pay nothing until we are able to accept gifts
  • Your price is locked from the day you sign on
